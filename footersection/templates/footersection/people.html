Patrons

Vallabh Bhanshali

Chairman, ENAM Securities
Founder, Desh Apnayen
Governing Body Member, FLAME University

Mr. Vallabh Bhanshali is a well-known thought leader, mentor and visionary investor businessman of India who has devoted his life to a range of social, national and spiritual fields. He is the Co-founder of ENAM Securities, the reputed investment Banking and Investment Group. His non-profit, Desh Apnayen Foundation works with Government and private schools to inculcate active citizenship at young age. He also started the platform - Truth Talks to highlight the practical benefits of a truthful life. He has served on the Board of Reserve Bank of India and on several policy making advisory committees.

Monik Sameer Koticha

Promoter, ASK and Wealth Management
Managing Trustee, Fortress Foundation,
Sameer Koticha Family Trust

Mr. Monik Sameer Koticha is part of the promoter family of ASK Group. Monik is also the managing director of Fortress Group (Family office of Sameer Koticha, Founder & Chairman of ASK Group) and oversees the investments in equity, real estate & private equity for the firm. He is an avid investor with a focus on bottom-up investments.

He is also responsible for family and corporate philanthropic initiatives and is actively involved with several causes in the areas of education, housing & nation building.

Founding Director

Yugank Goyal

Professor in Public Policy
FLAME University

Yugank joined in 2021 with a vision to build the Centre for Knowledge Alternatives and document India's districts. He studied engineering in India and later pursued Masters and PhD in law and economics from Europe as an Erasmus Mundus scholar. He was one of the founding members of O.P. Jindal Global University, and the Indian School of Public Policy. He is Member, Maharashtra Govt. NEP Steering Committee and Honorary Advisor, MITRA (Maharashtra Institution for Transformation). His research lies at the interesection of law and development, institutional economics and knowledge frameworks.
