PARTNERSHIPS

Partnerships

Collaborate With Us

We welcome partnerships with academic institutions, government bodies, NGOs, and local organizations that share our commitment to documenting and understanding India's districts. Being an ambitious project, we hope to cover all Indian districts over time and recognize the sheer scale of this work.

Why Partner With Us?

We hugely welcome collaborations to achieve this exercise of documentation and curation for more effective information dissemination and nation building. Our project aims to create decentralized repositories of knowledge in our ever-so-diverse country, moving beyond traditional ways of knowledge production and consumption.

Types of Partnerships

  • Research Collaborations: Joint fieldwork and data collection initiatives
  • Data Sharing Agreements: Access to government surveys and district-level statistics
  • District Documentation Projects: Comprehensive mapping of local culture and development
  • Educational Partnerships: Academic institutions contributing research and students
  • Technology Collaborations: Digital platforms and data visualization tools
  • Local Partnerships: Working with district administrations and local intellectuals

What We Offer Partners

This portal hopes to assist local administration in better policymaking. It will help familiarize new bureaucrats and local government officials with the district they govern, giving insightful information for better policy planning, allowing for data-based management of the district.

The portal is also likely to be of great utility for local entrepreneurs and businesses for richer market information. If the next level of development is hinged on small town aspirations, the portal will offer useful starting points.

Our Approach to Collaboration

We believe that small is beautiful (in the E.F. Schumacher way, but more closely in a Gandhian way). We believe that decentralized, polycentric thinking is the desirable attitude to not only understand but also govern India. We work with local partners who are storehouses of knowledge about their place.

Get in Touch

If you're interested in partnering with us to document your district or contribute to our research, please reach out to discuss potential collaboration opportunities. We need to give each place the power to define their identity and their own development agenda.

If you would like to partner with us for your district(s), please do write to us. Together, we can build our knowledge, the way we collectively want.
