Join Our Team

We are always looking for passionate researchers, data analysts, and content creators who share our vision of documenting India's rich cultural and developmental landscape at the district level.

What We Look For

We find people with fire in their belly, an insatiable curiosity about our own lands and freedom to excavate information, and put them together. This is where the magic starts.

  • Researchers passionate about local cultures and development
  • Data analysts skilled in government data interpretation
  • Content creators who can tell compelling local stories
  • Field workers comfortable with extensive district-level research
  • Local partners and intellectuals with deep knowledge of their places

Our Work Culture

We believe in decentralized, polycentric thinking. We don't theorize - we document India as it is. Our work involves extensive fieldwork, collecting stories and narratives, exploring vernacular texts, and working with local partners who are storehouses of knowledge about their place.

Current Opportunities

Please check back regularly for new opportunities or reach out to us if you're interested in contributing to our mission of creating knowledge about India by documenting its places at the district level.

How to Apply

Send your resume and a cover letter explaining your interest in our work to our recruitment team. We value curiosity, research skills, and a passion for understanding India's diverse districts. Tell us about your connection to local cultures and your experience with fieldwork or data analysis.
