Methodology

All the statistical and quantitative data that is displayed on the Portal is sourced from one or the other sources of the government of India. The explicit sources are generally mentioned against the data. Wherever not mentioned, we encourage you to bring it to our attention. The secondary sources used (for e.g., the gazetteers of British India) are mentioned at the end of each page.

The qualitative information comes from our own fieldwork, narratives by local partners and people from the respective districts, and even secondary (even vernacular literature). None of the ideas, narratives, explanations, documentation reflects the opinions or viewpoints of CKA or anyone employed within the CKA.

We also collect information from many of our users, who contribute to the information in the Portal. For the contribution that comes from our users, we conduct a quality check, and basic audit before we publish the information on the Portal. We do not conduct a due diligence or verify the content in detail if we do not have reasons to believe that the information is incorrect.

For correction of information on the Portal, we rely on users themselves to alert us to inconsistency, errors, or inaccuracies. We request the users to bring to our notice any discrepancy found in the Portal.
